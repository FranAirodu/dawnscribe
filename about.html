About DawnScribe

Where Stories Meet Art

A place built for creators and community — where authors and artists come together, and readers and art lovers are always welcome.

Join DawnScribe Explore

DawnScribe is the home for artists and authors alike.

We built this platform because we believe two of the most powerful creative forces — the written word and visual art — are stronger together. DawnScribe is designed for both talents to unite, collaborate, and build something neither could create alone. And for the readers and art lovers who appreciate those talents, this is your community too — a place to discover, connect, and grow alongside the people making the work you love.

What We Stand For

Built on Three Ideas

Original Creation

Every story and every piece of art on DawnScribe belongs to the person who made it. We exist to give original work a home — not to host copies, reposts, or imitations.

Collaboration

The best creative work often happens between people. DawnScribe makes it easy for writers and artists to find each other, work together, and build something bigger than either could alone.

Community

Creators grow when they have people around them who care. DawnScribe is a place to find your audience, make genuine connections, and be part of a community that takes creativity seriously.

Who DawnScribe Is For

A Place for Every Role

Authors

Publish your web novels chapter by chapter, build a readership, collaborate with artists to bring your worlds to life visually, and earn from your work when you're ready.

Artists

Share your original artwork, partner with authors on cover art and character designs, sell avatar items in the Marketplace, and find an audience that genuinely appreciates visual storytelling.

Readers

Follow your favourite authors, track your reading progress, discover new stories across every genre, and be part of the community that keeps creators motivated to keep writing.

Art Viewers

Browse and follow artists whose work moves you, collect avatar items crafted by the community, leave feedback that actually reaches the person who made the piece, and explore a gallery that grows every day.

Ready to Find Your Place?

Whether you have a story to tell, art to share, or just a love for the work others create — there's a place for you here.
