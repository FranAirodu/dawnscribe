Contact DawnScribe

Have a question, a report, or just want to reach us? Fill out the form and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

We're here to help.

Whether you need to report a content issue, ask about your account, flag a technical problem, or just have a question about how the platform works — this is the right place to reach us.

Account & Safety Issues

Compromised account, suspected ban evasion, harassment, or content that violates our guidelines — select the matching subject and describe what happened.

DMCA & Copyright

If you believe your copyrighted work has been posted without authorization, select "DMCA / Copyright" and include as much detail as possible. We respond to all valid takedown requests.

Technical Issues

Something not loading, a feature behaving unexpectedly, or a bug you want to report? Let us know what happened and which page or feature was involved.

General Questions

Anything else — platform questions, partnership enquiries, or feedback about DawnScribe — we read every message and appreciate you reaching out.

Response time We aim to respond to all messages within 72 hours. Urgent matters — such as safety reports or DMCA notices — are prioritized and reviewed as quickly as possible. DawnScribe staff will never ask for your password in any response.
