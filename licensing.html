Character Licensing

This system has been replaced. Art made from someone else’s novel now goes through the author directly.

What changed

  • Licensing is retired. It was built for the dress-up avatar system, which no longer exists. No licences were ever granted, so nothing has been taken away from anyone.
  • Approval happens per piece now. An artist makes a specific character or banner from your novel and submits it. You see the finished art, the description and exactly what you’d earn, then approve or decline with a note. Nothing goes live without your yes.
  • One split, every time: the artist keeps 55%, you get 20% as the novel’s author, and DawnScribe keeps 25%. If you make the art yourself, you keep 75%.
  • Free pieces earn nothing — an artist’s first character or banner is donated to the free randomizer. You still approve it, and readers of your novel may discover it that way.
  • Nothing to set up in advance. There’s no opt-in list to maintain. Requests come to you as they happen.

Where to go

  • Your Collab Inbox — characters and banners from your novels waiting on your approval.
  • Make a Character and Make a Banner — if you draw, you can make art from your own novel, or ask another author for theirs.
  • Payouts — what you’ve earned, and when you can withdraw it.
  • Terms, section 13a — the full revenue rules.
