Make a Character

A character is one illustrated figure from a novel on DawnScribe. Readers collect them, tap them to hear them talk, and build affection with them over time.

Donate first, then sell

  • Your first character goes into the free randomizer. Readers can win it with Embers, forever. Donating one unlocks selling characters and taking character commissions. A donated banner doesn't count — each kind needs its own donation.
  • After that, set a price between 50 and 5,000 Quills (100 Quills is about $1).
  • From your own novel: you keep 75% of every sale.
  • From someone else's novel: its author approves it first, then you keep 55% and the author gets 20%. DawnScribe keeps 25% either way.
  • Buyers keep a character forever — it's never retired from their collection.

The art you'll need

  • Full figure: 1200 × 1800 px (2:3), transparent PNG, feet at the bottom.
  • Portrait crop: 512 × 512 px, head centred so it still reads inside a small circle. This is what shows in comments and lists.
  • Four faces: neutral, pleased, annoyed and surprised, each 512 × 512. The head must not move between them — same eye line, size and position — or it visibly jumps when the character talks.
  • Your own original work only — no AI-generated, traced or borrowed art. No explicit or sexual content.
