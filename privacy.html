Your privacy matters to us.

DawnScribe collects only what is necessary to run the platform. We do not sell your personal data to advertisers or third parties, and we never will.

Section 1

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how DawnScribe ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and handles your personal information when you use our platform. It applies to all users — readers, authors, artists, and visitors who have not yet created an account.

By using DawnScribe, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. This policy should be read alongside our Terms of Service.

Section 3

How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect for the following purposes only:

  • To create and manage your account and verify your identity when you sign in.
  • To display your profile, published works, and activity to other users as the platform is designed to do.
  • To power features like Reading History, Following, caught-up status, and personalized feeds.
  • To process payments and manage your subscription where applicable.
  • To send essential communications — such as account verification, security alerts, or policy updates. We do not send marketing emails without your explicit consent.
  • To detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and violations of our Terms of Service.
  • To comply with legal obligations, including responding to valid legal requests.

We do not use your data to serve targeted advertisements. DawnScribe does not have an advertising business model and does not sell access to your information to any advertiser, now or in the future.

Section 4

DawnScribe does not use third-party tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics cookies. We do not participate in any cross-site tracking or behavioural advertising network.

Section 5

Sharing Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With other users — your username, display name, avatar, profile bio, and published content are visible to other users as part of normal platform operation. Your email address is never made public.
  • With Supabase — our database and authentication infrastructure provider. Supabase processes data on our behalf and is contractually bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations.
  • With our payment processor — when you subscribe or make a purchase, payment details are handled by a third-party payment provider. We do not store raw card numbers or payment credentials on our own servers.
  • With Vercel — our hosting provider, which serves the platform and may log basic request metadata such as IP addresses for security and performance purposes.
  • When required by law — we will disclose information if required to do so by a court order, subpoena, or other lawful legal process. We will notify affected users where we are legally permitted to do so.
  • To protect safety — we may share information with law enforcement if we believe it is necessary to prevent imminent harm to a person or to address illegal content, including content involving the exploitation of minors.

We will never share your email address, private messages, or personal account details with other users or third-party companies outside the circumstances described above.

Section 6

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, which provides encrypted database storage and enforces row-level security — meaning each user can only access their own private data through the platform.

  • Passwords are never stored in plain text. They are hashed using industry-standard encryption.
  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Data is never sent over unencrypted connections.
  • Profile images and cover art are stored in secure cloud storage buckets with access controls.
  • We regularly review and update our security practices.

While we implement strong protections, no internet-based system can guarantee complete security. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you promptly and take all reasonable steps to address it.

Section 7

Children & Minors

DawnScribe is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If we learn that a user under 13 has created an account, we will terminate it and delete the associated data promptly.

For users between 13 and 17, a parent or legal guardian must have given permission. We encourage parents and guardians to monitor their child's use of the platform and to review this Privacy Policy with them.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us immediately.

Section 8

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data on DawnScribe:

  • Access — you can view all profile information we hold about you through your account settings at any time.
  • Correction — you can update or correct your profile information, email address, and display name at any time.
  • Deletion — you may request deletion of your account and associated personal data. Some data may be retained for a limited period for legal or security purposes.
  • Data portability — upon request, we can provide you with a copy of your data in a readable format.
  • Withdrawal of consent — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time without affecting prior processing.
  • Opt-out of communications — you may opt out of non-essential communications at any time through your account settings.

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and California have additional rights under GDPR, UK GDPR, and the CCPA respectively. If these apply to you, please contact us directly.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us through the DawnScribe platform. We will respond within 30 days.

Section 9

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as long as is necessary to provide you with the Service.

  • If you delete your account, we will remove your personal profile data within 30 days.
  • Published content may be retained for a period after account deletion to allow any ongoing collaborations or marketplace transactions to be resolved, or may be removed at your request prior to deletion.
  • Records related to reported violations or legal matters may be retained longer for compliance purposes.
  • Anonymized, aggregated data — such as platform usage statistics — may be retained indefinitely as it cannot be linked to any individual.
  • LocalStorage data is stored on your own device and is not subject to our retention schedule. You may clear it at any time through your browser settings.
Section 10

Third-Party Services

DawnScribe uses a small number of carefully selected third-party services:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage. Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel — platform hosting and delivery. Vercel Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — serves the Cinzel and Lato typefaces. Google may log font request metadata. No personal account data is shared through this service.
  • Payment processor (to be confirmed when membership launches) — all payment data is handled by our provider under their own security and privacy standards. We will update this section with the specific provider name before the membership feature goes live.

DawnScribe does not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other advertising, behavioral tracking, or analytics tool that tracks users across the web.

Section 11

US State Privacy Rights

If you are a resident of California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, or another US state with applicable data privacy legislation, you may have additional rights including:

  • The right to know what personal information we have collected about you in the past 12 months.
  • The right to request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions.
  • The right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. Note: DawnScribe does not sell personal information.
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.
  • California residents additionally have the right to opt out of sharing personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising (which DawnScribe does not engage in).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us through the platform. We do not discriminate against users for exercising their privacy rights and will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

Section 12

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will notify users through the platform.

Your continued use of DawnScribe after any update constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

Section 13

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to exercise your data rights, or need to report a privacy concern, please contact the DawnScribe team through the platform's messaging system.

We take privacy matters seriously and will respond to all legitimate inquiries promptly.
