Link Type
📖 Sequel
Continues the established timeline and takes place after the original.
 ⏮ Prequel
Released after the original but set before it in the narrative timeline.
 🔀 Midquel
Takes place between events of an existing story.
 🔗 Interquel
Bridges the gap between two previously released stories or installments.
 🔄 Paraquel / Sidequel
Runs simultaneously alongside the original, often following different characters.
 🌀 Circumquel
Takes place partly before and partly after another story — wraps around it.
 🌿 Spin-Off
Set in the same universe but follows secondary characters on their own path.
 ✨ Spiritual Sequel
Heavily inspired by another work but has no official plot or universe connection.
 👴 Legacy Sequel
Made long after the original, shifting focus to new characters while keeping old ones.
 📌 Standalone Sequel
Set in the same universe but can be understood without reading the original.
 🔁 Reboot
Resets the continuity entirely — a fresh reimagining of the world and characters.
 📝 Remake
A direct retelling of an existing story, closely following the original.
 💭 What IF
A non-canon alternate timeline — what if things had gone differently?
 🎉 April Fools
Surprise us and your readers.
 🌐 Fan Translation
A reader-made translation of an existing story into another language. Links instantly — the original author gets notified, no approval needed.
