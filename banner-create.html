Make a Profile Banner

Upload a landscape image that other users can apply as their profile background. Once approved it appears in the Marketplace and on your profile Showcase.

Image requirements

  • Banners must be your original artwork — no AI-generated images, traced work, or assets you don't own rights to.
  • Recommended size: 1920×480 px (4:1 wide landscape). Minimum 1400×400 px. Larger is fine — anything wider than 1920 px is automatically scaled down to 1920 px on upload.
  • PNG, JPG or WebP, under 8 MB.
  • Banners span the full width of the window, so the crop changes with screen size — keep anything essential inside the middle 80% of the height, and away from the far left and right edges. On a wide monitor the top and bottom are trimmed; on a phone the sides are.
  • A banner is a backdrop, not a display piece: it sits behind the profile or story header, softly blurred and darkened towards the bottom. Bold shapes and colour read well; fine linework and small text do not.

Donate first, then sell

  • Your first banner goes into the free randomizer. Any reader can win it for a few Embers, forever. It's the best advert your work can get — and donating one unlocks selling banners and taking banner commissions.
  • After that, set a price between 100 and 3,000 Quills (100 Quills is about $1). Most banners sit around 300.
  • Your own work: you keep 75% of every sale.
  • Made for someone else's novel: pick the novel below. Its author has to approve it first, then you keep 55% and the author gets 20%. DawnScribe keeps 25% either way.
  • Earnings are tracked in real money and appear on your Payouts page.

Content rules

  • Allowed: dark, moody and dramatic work. A wounded or bloodied character is fine, and so is suggestive or sensual art.
  • Not allowed: graphic gore — dismemberment, mutilation, or blood as the subject rather than the mood.
  • Not allowed: explicit or erotic content, nudity, or anything sexualising a character who reads as a minor.
  • Not allowed: hateful imagery, real-world slurs or symbols, or harassment of a real person.
  • If you're unsure whether a piece crosses the line, submit it — a reviewer will tell you rather than penalise you.

Review

  • Every banner is reviewed before it goes live (typically 3–5 days).
  • If it's declined you'll get a reason, and you can edit and resubmit the same draft.
