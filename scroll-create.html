Give your Scroll a name, description, and a banner image that represents its theme. The banner will also be available as a profile background for buyers.

Creator Guidelines

  • Scrolls must be original art — no AI-generated images, traced work, or assets you don't own rights to.
  • No adult, graphic, or offensive content. Scrolls are visible to all ages.
  • Novel-linked Scrolls require the novelist's consent. You must have an approved collab on DawnScribe.
  • Your Scroll won't go live until reviewed and approved by the DawnScribe team.
  • You can test your Scroll privately before submitting.
