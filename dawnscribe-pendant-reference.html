DawnScribe — Prestige Pendant Artist Reference Sheet
Shape: Dawn Crest  ·  Style: Anime / Cel-shaded  ·  Version 1.0  ·  For commissioned artists
01 — Deliverable Specifications
File format
PNG with fully transparent background. No JPG, no WebP.
Canvas size
500 × 600 px — pendant artwork centered within this canvas. Minimum 20px padding on all sides from the canvas edge to the pendant silhouette.
Resolution
72 dpi screen export. Artwork should be created at 2× resolution (1000 × 1200 px) and exported down, so it looks sharp on retina displays.
Color mode
sRGB. Do not use CMYK. Ensure colors look correct on a dark background (#0d0d1a).
Layers
Please deliver both a flattened PNG and the original layered file (.psd / .clip / .procreate / .kra). Layered file allows us to make minor adjustments without a revision request.
Naming convention
Use the badge slug provided per commission. Example: bookworm.png, realm_walker.png, heartstring.png
Revisions included
2 rounds of revisions per badge. Please confirm the sketch/lineart stage before rendering final colors.
02 — The Dawn Crest Shape
1
The bail (loop at top) Do NOT draw this. It is rendered in code. Your artwork starts below the bail. Leave the top 80px of canvas clear.
2
The outer frame Do NOT draw the outer frame border or outline — this is where the gem tier color goes, applied by code. Your art fills the interior shape only.
3
The artwork area This is yours. Fill the interior of the crest shape with an illustration representing the badge theme. The shape is a flat-top arch with a slightly pointed base.
4
Sunrise ray motif (optional flourish) Small rays radiating from the top arc are part of the DawnScribe identity. You may incorporate these subtly into your artwork, or leave them to the frame.
5
Text zones (bottom) The gem name and badge name are rendered by code below the pendant. Do not include any text in your artwork.
03 — Art Style Guide
Overall Style

Anime / cel-shaded. Think collectible charm or magical pendant from an RPG. Clean linework, bold colors, cel shading with 2–3 shadow tones. Each pendant should feel like it could exist as a real wearable object.

Mood

Warm and slightly luminous. These are prestige rewards — they should feel earned and special. Avoid flat or muted palettes. A subtle inner glow on the key element (the badge icon/motif) is encouraged.

Background

The interior of the pendant can have a simple gradient or subtle texture (stone, fabric, sky). Keep it understated so the main motif reads clearly at small sizes.

✓ DO

  • Use strong, readable silhouettes — the motif must be clear at 86px wide
  • Cel-shade with 2–3 light levels
  • Add a small specular highlight on the main element
  • Keep the palette cohesive (2–3 dominant colors)
  • Reference DawnScribe gem colors for accents (see Section 05)
  • Leave clean edges — the shape mask is applied by code
  • Draw on a transparent background

✗ DON'T

  • Draw the outer frame/border — code handles this
  • Draw the bail (the loop at the top)
  • Include any text or gem name in the artwork
  • Use a white or solid colored background
  • Add a drop shadow (the code renders its own glow)
  • Use more than 4–5 colors in the illustration
  • Place important details near the very bottom point of the crest shape (gets clipped at small sizes)

06 — Example Badge Themes (per commission)

Each badge commission will include a brief like the examples below. These are starting points — artistic interpretation is welcome.

Bookworm

Motif: An open book with a glowing bookmark ribbon curling upward. Warm amber interior. Cozy, inviting.

Realm Walker

Motif: A glowing magic doorway or archway set into the crest. Cool teal/blue glow emanating from beyond the portal.

Storyteller

Motif: A quill pen mid-stroke, trailing ink that forms into stars or letters. Purple and gold palette.

Heartstring

Motif: A red rose wrapped around a small locket or glowing heart. Romantic, soft pinks and reds.

Dawnkeeper

Motif: A sunrise over a horizon line, rays fanning up. Warm golds and pinks fading into deep blue. This is the flagship badge — make it stunning.

Star Seeker

Motif: A rocket or telescope silhouetted against a starfield. Deep navy interior, bright stars, cool blue tones.
