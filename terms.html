Section 14

Purchases, Lorescout & Lorekeeper

All core features of DawnScribe, including the Lorebook, are free to use. The paid products are Quills, purchased as one-time bundles, and two optional writing tools for authors, each available as a monthly subscription, a one-time pass, or a one-time block of reads:

  • Lorescout compares your chapters with the facts on your Lorebook cards and points out possible contradictions with them. It uses a smaller AI model that does only this task, and it can only check facts your cards contain.
  • Lorekeeper does the same check twice using a larger AI model, and also points out new facts that could be filed on your cards, unresolved threads, characters acting against their cards, and repeated scenes. It includes manuscript export.

Prices are shown before you pay. If you hold reads of both kinds, Lorekeeper reads are used first.

One-Time Purchases. Quill bundles and Lorescout and Lorekeeper passes are single, one-off transactions at a stated price. They do not renew and there is nothing to cancel. The reads a pass gives you do not expire; any extras that come with it, such as manuscript export with a Lorekeeper pass, last 30 days.

Lorekeeper Subscriptions. A Lorescout or Lorekeeper subscription renews each month at the stated price until you cancel it. You can cancel at any time from the Marketplace in one step; you keep Lorekeeper until the end of the period you have already paid for, and you are not charged again. Cancelling has no effect on your stories, your Lorebook, or your readers. The only recurring charge DawnScribe ever makes is a Lorescout or Lorekeeper subscription you started yourself — if you see any other recurring charge from us, contact us immediately.

Lorekeeper Reads. Each time Lorekeeper reads a chapter whose text has changed, one read is used from your balance. Reading exactly the same text again is free, and a read that fails for any reason is not charged.

What Lorescout and Lorekeeper Are, and Are Not. Both are automated, AI-assisted readers. It compares your chapters with your Lorebook and points out possible contradictions, unresolved threads, and similar issues. It will not find every problem, and some of what it points out will not be a problem at all. Its findings are suggestions only: nothing in your story or your Lorebook changes unless you choose it. Lorekeeper is not a professional editing, proofreading, or fact-checking service, and responsibility for your work remains yours. To improve its accuracy, findings that authors mark as wrong may be reviewed by DawnScribe without identifying the author.

Fees and Billing. All prices are in U.S. dollars unless otherwise stated. Payments are processed by a third-party payment processor; DawnScribe does not receive or store your full card details. You must be authorised to use the payment method you provide.

Delivery. Quills are credited to your account once the payment processor confirms the transaction. If a payment is later reversed, charged back, or refunded, DawnScribe may remove the corresponding Quills from your balance, and may remove items purchased with them or suspend the account where the reversal appears fraudulent.

Refunds. Quill purchases and Lorekeeper purchases are final and non-refundable except where required by applicable law, or at DawnScribe's discretion in the case of a demonstrable billing error. Quills that have already been spent, and Lorekeeper reads that have already been used, cannot be refunded; a Lorekeeper finding that turns out to be mistaken is not in itself a billing error. If you believe a charge was made in error, contact DawnScribe within 14 days.

Price and Bundle Changes. DawnScribe may change bundle prices, bundle contents, and the Quill cost of platform features at any time. Changes are not retroactive: Quills already purchased are not devalued or removed, and a completed purchase is not affected by a later price change.
