Currency Balances
Accounts whose Ember or Quill balance does not match the ledgers that should explain it. Only accounts with something worth looking at are shown by default, worst first — switch to "everyone" to browse the full list. Click a username to message them.

How to read this. Expected is lifetime earned + received + granted, minus everything spent. Unexplained is the balance minus that figure. A positive number means someone holds more than the records account for; a negative one means the ledger claims more than they hold.

Risk ranks how much an account is worth looking at. Quill mismatches score highest because Quills are bought with real money; Ember gaps, heavy earning over very few chapters, and holding a withdrawable payout balance all add to it. Why spells out what triggered it.

A flag is a prompt, not a verdict. Most drift is an honest bug rather than cheating — click the username to message the account and ask before assuming anything.

24h / 7d are Embers awarded for reading in that window, and Chapters is how many distinct chapters have ever paid out. A large 24h figure spread over very few chapters is the shape Ember farming takes.
