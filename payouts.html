How Artist Scroll revenue works

  • When a reader unlocks an Artist Scroll with Quills, the sale splits 60% to the Artist, 30% to the Novelist, and 10% to DawnScribe.
  • An Artist Scroll can only be sold once both the Artist and Novelist approve the listing.
  • Your USD balance accumulates from Quill sales at the exchange rate active at time of purchase — currently $7.00 per 1,000 Quills earned.

Character royalties

How character licensing royalties work

  • When an artist creates a cosmetic item using one of your licensed characters, you earn 30% of every sale and the artist earns 50%. DawnScribe takes 20%.
  • If you are both the character author and the cosmetic artist, you earn 80% of every sale.
  • Royalties accumulate in your USD balance and pay out monthly alongside your other earnings.
