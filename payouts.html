How Artist Scroll revenue works

  • When a reader unlocks an Artist Scroll with Quills (DawnScribe's paid currency), the sale splits 60% to the Artist, 30% to the Novelist, and 10% to DawnScribe.
  • An Artist Scroll can't be sold until both the Artist and the Novelist whose work it's based on approve the listing.
  • Balances below are tracked in Quill-equivalent value. Real-money payout via Stripe is planned — for now this is a running ledger of what you're owed.
